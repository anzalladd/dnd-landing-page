type: "UX Audit, Web Design & Web Development"
startingPoint: "Trovant was founded to become the leading distribution solution for healthcare users. Since then, they’ve expanded significantly. Set to express their mission and position in the ever-evolving healthcare industry, Trovant approached us to elevate its brand. Following a thorough strategy phase, we helped them design a new visual identity, build a new website, and develop an industry-leading B2B digital healthcare product."
outcome: "With this rebrand, we aimed to enable users to experience healthcare distribution in perfect flow. In close collaboration with the Trovant design team, we crafted a new tone of voice and messaging framework that emphasises Trovant’s effortless products, but also sets the tone for the future. Avoiding worn out tropes, we went for an expressive visual identity that is enhanced by vibrant illustrations and a custom typeface. Working from the outside in, we rebuild the entire brand, introduced a new design system and implemented UI to support exciting new user experiences across all surfaces, including the website and a new product."
